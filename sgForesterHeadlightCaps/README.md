# Vented Headlight Bulb Caps — 2006–2008 Subaru Forester (SG)

**Extended, louvered rear headlight caps that make room for LED bulbs** in the
2006–2008 Subaru Forester (SG facelift). Most LED low-beam bulbs carry a cooling
fan on their tail that doesn't fit under the stock rear cap — these caps extend
the dome to clear the fan, vent it with downward-angled louvers, and give the
LED's voltage box a wire slot so it can live outside the housing.

Designed and street-proven by SMITHTRONIC. Now open source: print it, build it,
remix it.

![Installed cap with louvered vents](gallery/cap_installed_closeup.jpg)

## Why it works

- **Extended design** — the dome adds the depth that rear-fan LED bulbs need;
  stock caps simply don't have the room.
- **Louvered vents** — fresh air for the bulb fans, angled downward so water
  sheds off instead of dripping in.
- **External wire slot** — the LED's voltage management box won't fit inside the
  housing; the slot routes its wire out so the box rides externally.
- **Factory locking** — three quarter-turn tabs, one oversized so the cap only
  goes on one way, plus the **stock O-ring** reused for sealing.
- **One part, both sides** — left and right take the same cap; print two.
- **ASA construction** — heat resistance for life above a hot bulb, UV and
  weather resistance for life in an engine bay.

## Print it

ASA strongly recommended (the caps sit over hot LED bulbs). Both caps print on
one plate in about the footprint of a coffee mug: 0.08 mm layers, 4 walls, 40 %
Rectilinear infill, manually painted supports under the louvers. Full details in
[`docs/print-settings.md`](docs/print-settings.md).

## Install it

Seven steps, no tools beyond reaching the back of the housings — the whole
procedure is in [`docs/install-guide.md`](docs/install-guide.md). The short
version: pull the stock caps, move their O-rings to the printed ones, install
your LED bulbs, route the voltage box wire through the slot, and twist-lock the
caps in (large tab first).

**Compatibility:** designed for aftermarket LED headlight bulbs with rear
cooling fans (the ones used here were H1-size Sylvania LEDs with external
voltage boxes). Ventilation and clearance are improved, but full waterproofing
is not guaranteed.
