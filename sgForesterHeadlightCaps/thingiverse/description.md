## Description (paste below the line into the Thingiverse description field)

Extended, vented rear headlight caps for the **2006-2008 Subaru Forester (SG
facelift)** — made for LED low-beam bulbs whose rear cooling fans don't fit under
the stock caps. Designed and street-proven by SMITHTRONIC; now open source.

**Why this exists:**
Stock rear caps leave no room for the cooling fan on the back of most LED
headlight bulbs, and the LED's voltage box has nowhere to live. These caps extend
the dome to clear the fan, add louvered vents so the fan can actually move air,
and carry an external wire slot so the voltage box rides outside the housing.

**What you print (ASA strongly recommended):**
- 2x headlight_cap — the same part fits both sides of the car; the plate project
  prints the pair in one go
- The CAD source (STEP) is included too — remix the dome or louvers around your
  own bulb in Fusion, SolidWorks, FreeCAD, or Onshape

ASA because the caps live over hot bulbs in the engine bay. The production
profile is in the 3MF and in print-settings.md on GitHub: 0.08 mm layers, 4
walls, 40% Rectilinear infill, manually painted supports under the louvers, 260C
nozzle, High Temp Plate at 100C. Saved with Bambu Studio 2.3 — use 2.3+.

**What else you need:**
- The O-rings from your stock caps — peel them off and reuse them; nothing to buy
- Your LED bulbs (the ones used here were H1-size Sylvania LEDs with rear fans
  and external voltage boxes)

**Install:**
1. Remove the stock caps (pull the battery on the driver's side and the fender
   intake baffle on the passenger side if you need the room)
2. Carefully peel the O-ring off each stock cap
3. Install the LED bulbs per their manufacturer's directions
4. Route the harness connector through the O-ring
5. Guide the voltage box wire through the cap's slot, then loop it back through
6. Slip the O-ring over the printed cap
7. Each cap has three locking tabs — one is larger so it only fits one way. Line
   up the large tab and twist to lock it into the housing.

**Notes:**
- The louvers angle downward to shed water, but full waterproofing is not
  guaranteed
- Check your bulb's fan diameter — the extended dome clears the fans used here,
  but oversized fan assemblies exist
