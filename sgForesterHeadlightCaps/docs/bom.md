# Bill of materials

The caps are almost the whole list — this design deliberately reuses the stock
sealing hardware.

| Item | Qty | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Printed vented cap | 2 | [`models/`](../models/) | Same part both sides; ASA strongly recommended ([print settings](print-settings.md)) |
| O-ring | 2 | **reused from your stock caps** | Carefully peel it off the OEM cap and slip it onto the printed one — nothing to buy |
| LED low-beam bulbs with rear cooling fans | 2 | your choice | The reason this cap exists; the ones used here were H1-size Sylvania LEDs with external voltage boxes |

Nothing else: no fasteners, no adhesive, no heat-set inserts. The cap locks into the
headlight housing with the factory quarter-turn tabs, and the O-ring does the sealing.

**Fitment reminder:** 2006–2008 Subaru Forester (SG facelift) headlight housings.
Check your LED's voltage box and wiring against the cap's external wire slot — the
box rides *outside* the cap (it won't fit inside the housing), with the wire looped
through the slot.
